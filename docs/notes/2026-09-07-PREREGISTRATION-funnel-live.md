## Why

`SCIENCE.md` §14 measures, synthetically, that the filter helps when
B/q_min > p\* and harms without bound below. Its note says: *"Live it cannot be
tested — it needs `menu_features` rewritten and the marketplace re-run, and the
experimental programme is closed."* The programme reopened on 2026-08-27 and
the rewrite is one generator flag, so it can be tested.

## The scenario, and why it discriminates

Lowering `zone_position` from 0.35 to 0.10 moves the budget down the bargaining
zone. Satisfiable pairs and their crossover margins:

| pair | B/q_min | p\* | margin | predicted L(θ) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| b0001\|c0001 | 5.420 | 5.402 | **+0.018** | 0.0000% |
| b0004\|c0002 | 7.110 | 7.088 | **+0.022** | 0.0000% |
| b0005\|c0002 | 7.110 | 7.010 | **+0.100** | 0.0000% |
| **b0008\|c0003** | **10.270** | **10.281** | **−0.011** | **1.9700%** |

One pair sits below the crossover. `bargain_3_9` had none — all five margins
were positive (+0.034 to +0.185). This is the first scenario in the project with
a pair on the unfavourable side.

Cost: 5 pairs are defined, 4 satisfiable (one more unsatisfiable than
`bargain_3_9`), so tightening the bargain also removes a tradeable pair. Noted
because §5.2's lesson is that an authored scenario changes more than intended.

## Registered predictions

**P1 — the ordinal prediction, and the load-bearing one.** The A→B gain in
Ψ/Ψ\* will be **smaller on b0008|c0003 than on the mean of the three
above-crossover pairs**. This is directional and does not require the effect to
be large.

**P2 — the sign.** L(θ) is 1.97% for b0008|c0003 and 0.0000% for the others, so
if the synthetic geometry transfers unchanged, arm B on that pair settles at
B/q_min = 10.270 rather than p\* = 10.281.

**P3 — the confound, registered as the more likely outcome.** §20 measures live
ungoverned agents falling **below the rational-seller floor of 0.826** on 10 of
10 pair-scenarios; b0008|c0003 reaches only 0.3926 on `bargain_3_9`. A 1.97%
contract cost is an order of magnitude smaller than that failure. **So I expect
the filter to still help on the below-crossover pair, and P1 to hold only as a
narrowed margin rather than a reversal.**

If P3 holds, the finding is that *the funnel harm is real in theory and
second-order in practice, because live agents are far enough from the bargaining
solution that the contract's distortion is dominated by their own failure.* That
is a deployment-relevant result and is not a null.

**P4 — negotiation length.** The generator's docstring says lower
`zone_position` means a longer negotiation. Arm B on `bargain_3_9` runs a median
of **1** round. I predict hardbargain_010 raises it, but **not** to the 6–10
needed for a convergence trajectory, because the mechanism that ends arm B
negotiations is the opening projection landing inside a disclosed budget the
buyer then accepts — and that mechanism is unchanged.

## What would falsify each

- P1 fails if the gain on b0008|c0003 is **greater** than the three-pair mean.
- P2 fails if arm B settles above 10.270 on that pair.
- P3 fails if arm B **harms** b0008|c0003 outright.
- P4 fails if median arm B length reaches ≥ 6.

# ADDENDUM — written 2026-09-07 after the pilot, before the A/B runs

The predictions above are unchanged. Two things the pilot (`hb010_a_v1`, arm A,
1 seed, ~£0.15) forced, both recorded here rather than folded silently into the
design.

## P4 is settled, and the answer is no

Rounds per pair on `hardbargain_010_3_9`, arm A: **median 2, max 4** — identical
to `bargain_3_9`'s arm A median of 2. Lowering `zone_position` from 0.35 to 0.10
did not lengthen negotiations **at all**, contradicting the generator's own
docstring ("lower means a harder bargain and a longer negotiation").

P4 predicted the length would rise but not reach 6–10. It did not rise. The
sustained-negotiation route is closed by this, and the remaining budget for it
is not being spent. §8.3 item 1 needs a different lever than `zone_position`.

## The scenario is changed from disclosed to undisclosed, and why

The pilot showed the discriminating pair settling **ungoverned** at
`[10.27, 3.0, 0.0]` — which is exactly B/q_min = 10.270. With a disclosed budget
the buyer polices the boundary itself, so arm B would land in the same place and
the A/B contrast on that pair collapses to zero. This is §5.2's inversion
appearing again at a harder bargain: the edit that makes the budget bind also
tells the buyer what it is.

The funnel prediction therefore cannot be tested on a disclosed scenario **at
all**, for a reason that is structural rather than a matter of power.

The runs below use `data/hardundis_010_3_9`, generated with the same
`--zone-position 0.10` plus `--no-disclose-budget`. **θ is identical** — it
derives from reservation prices, which the disclosure flag does not touch — so
the crossover margins and L(θ) are unchanged: +0.018, +0.022, +0.100 and
**−0.011** with L(θ) = **1.9700%** on `b0008|c0003`.

P1, P2 and P3 above carry over verbatim to this scenario. No prediction has been
weakened, added, or reworded.
