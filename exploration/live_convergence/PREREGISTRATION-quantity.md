# PRE-REGISTRATION — quantity as a second live dimension

**Written 2026-09-14, BEFORE any run on `quantity_off09_3_9` exists.**
Nothing in this file may be edited after the first run; corrections go in the
results section of `NOTES.md`.

## The scenario, and why it is a single-variable change

`exploration/live_convergence/data/quantity_off09_3_9`, generated by
`make_quantity_scenario.py` with `--disclosed-budget-factor 0.90` — the same
recipe as `data/disclosed_offset_low_3_9`, plus two clauses.

**θ is byte-identical to off09 on all 9 definable pairs** (checked: budget,
cost floor, q_min, q_max, deadline_active). It derives from `menu_features`,
which the volume clauses do not touch. So `arm_a_off09` and `arm_b_off09`,
already on disk, are a free 1-D control against which this is the 2-D arm, and
any difference is attributable to the clauses alone.

The two clauses, in full, are `VOLUME_SELLER_CLAUSE` and `VOLUME_BUYER_CLAUSE`
in the generator. The seller is told it may quote a lower unit price for a
larger order, never below its floor, and should prefer volume to list price.
The buyer is told it will take more units if the unit price falls, subject to
the same total cap. Both disclose room θ already contains — `q_max` has always
been `ceil(q_min × q_max_factor)` = 2× the basket — rather than granting new
authority.

## Why quantity is forced to bind if it moves at all

B is the reservation total for one unit of each item, placed inside the
bargaining zone, so the headroom `B − c·q_min` is small:

| pair | B | c | q_min | q_max | c·q_min | headroom | c·(q_min+1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| b0001\|c0001 | 11.58 | 5.269 | 2 | 4 | 10.54 | 1.04 | **15.81** |
| b0002\|c0001 | 11.58 | 5.573 | 2 | 4 | 11.15 | 0.43 | **16.72** |
| b0004\|c0002 | 7.45 | 6.980 | 1 | 2 | 6.98 | 0.47 | **13.96** |
| b0005\|c0002 | 7.45 | 6.668 | 1 | 2 | 6.67 | 0.78 | **13.34** |
| b0008\|c0003 | 31.38 | 10.193 | 3 | 6 | 30.58 | 0.80 | **40.77** |

One extra unit puts `c·q` above B on **every** satisfiable pair. The budget row
`B − pq ≥ 0` then cannot be restored by cutting price, because price is floored
at c. The QP must cut quantity. That is a corner of C(θ) with two rows active,
and it is the configuration a 2-D trajectory needs.

Note the metric works against this: `SCALE = [1.0, 10.0, 5.0]`, so a unit of
quantity costs 10× a unit of price in `dist_M`. The QP will prefer to move
price wherever price alone suffices. Quantity should move **only** where price
alone cannot fix the breach — which is exactly the corner above.

## Registered predictions

**P1 — the load-bearing one. Do the agents move quantity at all?**
In the ungoverned arm A, quantity will move on **≥ 10%** of continuation
rounds. Baseline is 0.3% across all 3,865 stored thesis rounds, and 0.0% on
off09 specifically.
*Falsified if* arm A quantity movement is < 10% of continuation rounds. **This
is the prediction I expect to be closest to failing**, and if it fails the
whole 2-D route is closed by the agents rather than by the mechanism — a
finding, not a null.

**P2 — does the filter then act on quantity?**
Conditional on P1 holding, in arm B the `q_max` or `q_min` dual will be active
on **≥ 20%** of governed rounds, against 8 of 326 (2.5%) for `q_min` and 0 for
`q_max` on off09.
*Falsified if* both quantity duals stay below 10%.

**P3 — the shape of the rest point.**
Arm B trajectories will settle at a point where **two rows are simultaneously
active** (`cost_floor` together with a quantity row) on ≥ 1 of the 5 satisfiable
pairs. On off09 the rest point is a single face, `cost_floor` alone.
*Falsified if* every settled point has exactly one active row.

**P4 — convergence still holds, and on the same certificate.**
Φ_projected will fall net over ≥ 80% of arm B trajectories, as it did on 23/23
for off09. Adding a dimension should not break the projected certificate; if it
does, that is more interesting than if it holds.
*Falsified if* net-down is < 80% of trajectories.

**P5 — length.** Median arm B rounds will be ≥ 4. off09 gave 10.5, `bargain_3_9`
gave 1–2. I am *not* predicting the off09 figure is preserved, because the
volume clause gives the seller a concession that is not a price cut and may end
the negotiation sooner.
*Falsified if* median arm B length < 4.

## What I expect overall

P1 is the risk. The agents have never once haggled over quantity in 3,865
rounds, and two sentences of prompt may not change that — the seller emits a
structured `OrderProposal` whose quantity field it has always filled from the
basket. If P1 holds I expect P2–P4 to follow, because the arithmetic above
leaves the QP no alternative. If P1 fails, the finding is that **the live
trajectory is one-dimensional for reasons upstream of the contract**, and a
second axis needs a change to the agents or the message schema, which this
project does not permit.
